# Weekly Market Recap – USD Rates & FOMC Reaction

## Overview

This project presents a **weekly macroeconomic recap** focused on U.S. interest rates and inflation expectations following the FOMC meeting. It simulates a strategy desk note written for internal or client distribution at a financial institution, integrating real market data, flow commentary, and actionable positioning ideas.

## Objective

To provide a structured, data-driven summary of key market developments in:
- **U.S. Treasury yields**
- **Inflation expectations (5Y breakeven)**
- **FOMC policy shifts**
- **Trading flows and strategy positioning**

## Summary Highlights

- **10-Year Treasury Yield:** Rose from ~1.30% to 1.46% during the week
- **Inflation Expectations:** 5-year breakeven increased to 2.48%
- **FOMC Outlook:** Hawkish dot plot shift; market repriced to expect 1–2 hikes by December 2022
- **Market Flows:**
  - Fast money paid fixed in short-dated swaps
  - Real money added duration in long-end Treasuries

## Tactical Strategy View

- **Tactical Position:** Receive fixed in 5-year swaps to express curve steepening view
- **Defensive Hedge:** Long 10-year Treasuries as a risk-off buffer
- **Bias:** Modestly steepening curve on sticky inflation + resilient growth
